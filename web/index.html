      How to play

      Each turn both players secretly lock in one piece, then both are revealed. Your opponent's pieces stay hidden. You are never told what they hold or have lost, so the game is about memory, odds and reading your opponent.

      You win when

      • Your opponent's last Monarch is taken.

      If both Monarchs fall in the same round it is a draw. Having no piece that can beat the opponent's does not end the game.

      Multiplayer (2 to 6)

      Everyone secretly picks one piece. Then your piece fights every other player's piece at once, as separate clashes judged only from what was revealed. A piece that loses (or ties) any clash is destroyed, even if it won another. A piece that wins or draws every clash returns to hand. You are out when your last Monarch falls; the last player left wins. If the last players fall together it is a draw. When you pick the Joker you also choose, in secret, which player's piece it copies. It becomes that piece for good, draws with that player and fights everyone else as the copied piece.

      Equal pieces destroy each other. The winning piece returns to hand and can be used again; only the Dragon is spent. A Joker becomes the piece it meets; both then return to hand. The Joker can never be destroyed before it transforms.
